What I want from the app:

I attend a lecture for a class.
After the lecture, I provide resources to honed such as slides, my notes, powerpoint, pdf, textbook etc. (proof of concept with only slides for now)
I tell the app how long that lecture took in class, as a baseline for how well I should know the content. example: 'In lecture today (50 mins) we went over slides 10-15 of this slide deck'.
An algorithm is in place that will schedule (based on my desired study time per day) and allocate studying those resources over a set interval.
During the study session(s), the slides should be presented to me in a built in viewer, to display them in their original form.  
In example 50 minute study session, after 40 minutes with the slides, or when I decide I'm ready, a quiz based on the information in the slides is given to me via AI feature.
The results of this quiz influence how much more studying of this specific section needs. Good grades lead to lighter quizes less and less often. Bad grades will lead to heavier quizes and potential redisplaying of lecture content until quiz results improve.


What the slide view should look like:

Display slides from powerpoint, sheets, or other common slide types.
Tools to add persistent notes and highlighting text and/or areas.
AI features, such as explaining a concept in more details, or answering a question about a certain part/concept.


What the user experience should be:

	First login:
		Create an account, with email and password, maybe google sign in
		User is brought through a tutorial that shows them the features of the app.
		* It shows them how to add classes to your account.
		* A class should contain how many lectures per week, how long they are and on which days.
		* Optionally add start and end dates for the class
		* Optianally store the dates of midterms and final exams
		Get the user to add content from thier class
		See if the user would like to do their first review of their class material

	
	Normal use case:
		The app should know what time and day classes happen, and promt you for material covered. (example: 'we covered slides 21-30' or 'Here are the slides we covered today'[+upload])
		It should take you to the main screen, which should be a very concise checklist of things to do for the day
		A big play button that takes you through all of the checklist items. (the study and quiz loop)
		
		
		
Future feature ideas:
	- Add backlog of missed content, to "catch up" after missing a chunk of a class. This would add a cramming of information to each day.
	- Upload practice tests for the ai tool to make more relevant test questions,adjust quizing, and/or full practice tests
	- Leaderboards to provide hits of dopamine
